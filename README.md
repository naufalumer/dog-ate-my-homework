One line: the excuse a dog gives after being caught eating the homework. The dog did not eat it out of hunger or spite — it ate it to keep its human home, because homework is what takes the human away to school. The plan worked. The dog is proud.

> I ate your homework so school couldn't take you away. Look, you're still here. You're welcome.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`) and prints it.
